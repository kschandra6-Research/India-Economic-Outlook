## Platform

web

## Users

Inferred from the request: readers reviewing Indian economic outlook projections, likely analysts or decision-makers comparing agency forecasts.

## Product Purpose

An interactive, standalone dashboard that makes the workbook's India FY2027–28 projections and FY2026–27 comparison easy to scan.

## Positioning

The dashboard brings growth, inflation, drivers, policy outlook, and workbook references together for side-by-side comparison.

## Operating Context

Inferred: used in a browser to review and compare the supplied spreadsheet.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The attached workbook is the only source of economic figures and source descriptions. Missing values remain marked as not in source. Agency forecasts are not normalized into unsupported aggregate claims.

## Evidence on Hand

`India Economic Outlook Projections for FY 2027-2028.xlsx`, including the `Table 1` forecast data and `Source References` sheet.

## Product Principles

- Preserve the workbook's figures and qualifiers.
- Make agency comparisons easy to inspect.
- Distinguish FY2027–28 projections from FY2026–27 estimates.
- Do not fabricate or infer unavailable data.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Inferred: use responsive layouts, semantic controls, keyboard access, and readable contrast.
